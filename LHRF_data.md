### Official Dataset Sources

The raw images are not hosted in this repository due to licensing restrictions. Please obtain the datasets from their official providers:

*   **CVC-ClinicDB & CVC-ColonDB:** Available from the Computer Vision Center (CVC) at UAB. Access requires registration and a signed Data Use Agreement.
    *   CVC-ClinicDB: https://polyp.grand-challenge.org/site/Polyp/CVCClinicDB/
    *   CVC-ColonDB: http://vi.cvc.uab.es/colon-qa/cvccolondb/
*   **Kvasir-SEG:** Available from Simula Research Laboratory (Open Access for research).
    *   https://datasets.simula.no/kvasir-seg/
*   **Kvasir v2:** Available from Simula Research Laboratory.
    *   https://datasets.simula.no/kvasir/
